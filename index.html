    PATIENT CONTACT

    The first minutes are yours.

    You are the medical first responder. You get there first, you work the patient until ALS arrives, then you work for the medic — on scene and in the back of the rig.

    Everything runs on a clock: the ALS ETA, your compressor's arms, your oxygen, the drive to the hospital, and the bridges in between.

    Guided lists every step. Recall hides the list. Chaos adds things going wrong.

    When the medic arrives, you'll help put the patient on the cardiac monitor — and turn on Sound in the menu for monitor beeps, the CPR metronome, and AED voice prompts.

    A training aid built on current adult BLS guidelines. Your Medical Control Authority's protocols are what you follow on a real call. Units, hospitals, and equipment behavior are generic or simulated. Version 0.12.1 · prototype.
